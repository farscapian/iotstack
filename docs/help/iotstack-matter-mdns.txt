iotstack matter mdns — Check chip-tool operational mDNS

Checks whether a device on the chip-tool Matter fabric is visible on this host
via _matter._tcp (avahi-browse). Use after commissioning to confirm operational
discovery before opening the HA handoff window.

Usage:
  iotstack matter mdns [node-id]

Arguments:
  [node-id]   Fabric node ID to look up (default: 1)

The instance name is <compressed-fabric-id>-<16-digit-node-id>, saved after
commission (e.g. 5FD0DB16AB3C3FBE-0000000000000001). This is separate from HA
Matter instances (AC524104A0E54AC9-...).

Examples:
  iotstack matter mdns
  iotstack matter mdns 1

Help:
  iotstack matter mdns help
  iotstack matter help
