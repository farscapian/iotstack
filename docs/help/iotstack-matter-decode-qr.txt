iotstack matter decode-qr -- Extract MT: payload from a Matter QR image

Decodes a Matter QR code image and prints only the MT: setup payload string to
stdout (suitable for piping or pasting into other commands).

Usage:
  iotstack matter decode-qr <path-to-image>

Arguments:
  <path-to-image>   JPG, PNG, or other format supported by zbarimg

Dependencies:
  zbar-tools (zbarimg): apt install zbar-tools

Examples:
  iotstack matter decode-qr ~/qr-code.jpg
  iotstack matter decode-qr ~/Downloads/device-qr.png
  MT_PAYLOAD=$(iotstack matter decode-qr device.png)
  iotstack matter commission "$(iotstack matter decode-qr device.png)"
  iotstack matter commission MT:CUO000QV17VHCJ3.A00

Help:
  iotstack matter decode-qr help
