iotstack matter -- Matter device commissioning

Commission Matter-over-Thread devices with chip-tool and hand them off to Home
Assistant. Subcommands cover attestation trust setup, per-device commissioning,
and operational mDNS checks on the chip-tool fabric.

Subcommands:

  commission            Commission a device to Thread and hand off to Home Assistant
  configure-trust-store   Interactive PAA/CD trust store setup for chip-tool
  decode-qr <image>     Print MT: payload from a Matter QR code image
  mdns [node-id]        Check operational mDNS for one chip-tool fabric node

Typical workflow:
  1. iotstack matter configure-trust-store     # once per vendor/network
  2. iotstack matter commission <pairing-code> # per device

Usage:
  iotstack matter commission [-f] <qr-image>|<manual-pairing-code>|<MT:payload>
  iotstack -v matter commission <manual-pairing-code>   # global verbose
  iotstack matter configure-trust-store [manual-pairing-code|MT:payload]
  iotstack matter decode-qr <path-to-image>

Examples:
  iotstack matter configure-trust-store
  iotstack matter configure-trust-store 0167-951-2626
  iotstack matter commission ~/qr-code.jpg
  iotstack matter commission 0167-951-2626
  iotstack -v matter commission 0167-951-2626
  iotstack matter decode-qr ~/qr-code.jpg

Help:
  iotstack matter help
  iotstack matter commission help
  iotstack matter configure-trust-store help
  iotstack matter decode-qr help
  iotstack matter mdns help
