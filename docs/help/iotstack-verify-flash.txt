iotstack verify-flash -- Compare on-device flash checksums to build artifacts

Reads bootloader and firmware regions over USB and compares checksums to the
compiled build in yamls/.esphome/build/<role>/.

Usage:
  iotstack verify-flash <role> <tty>

Arguments:
  role              Device role (e.g. failsafe, bleproxy)
  tty               Serial port (e.g. /dev/ttyACM0)

Example:
  iotstack verify-flash failsafe /dev/ttyACM0
